## Fixed Occupations

For a system with a discrete band gap, electron occupations are represented by the **Heaviside Step Function:**
$$ 
f_{nk} = 1 if \epsilon_{nk} < \epsilon_F \n f_{nk} = 0 if \epsilon_{nk} > \epsilon_F 
$$
